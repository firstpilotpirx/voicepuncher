## Context

See proposal.md § Why. Since the proposal was filed, `command-spotting-stall-handling` landed: `confirming.rs` is now 1 153 lines (951 code lines in `app/tests/size_baseline.toml`), `apply_answer` 92 lines, `close_segment` 66, `place` 42, `Session` 28 fields; the line numbers in § Why are older than that. The preparation (`orchestration.yaml` § prepared) settled every open point: a `voice-commands` crate, an optional field in the existing selection message, a trailing-words table, a kept branch with a comment for a mishearing that cannot become a row, and a `confirming/` directory.

What the code holds today, by kind:

- **Command declarations, three times:** `matcher/placed.rs` (`FORMS`, `STANDBY`, `DICTATING`, `accepted_after`), `matcher.rs` (`ALIASES`, which repeats the forms and the mishearings "presenter", "star dictation", "stuck dictation", "capsule dictation"), `keywords.txt` (the keyword lines), and the app (`VoiceCommand::STANDBY`, `DICTATING`, `phrase()`, and the forms of `command_words.rs`).
- **Mishearings in code:** "and restart" for "enter start" (`placed.rs::ends_with_and_restart` and its branch in `chain_from`); "Let's start." for "restart" (already a table, `NEAR_MISSES` in `matcher.rs`, without its recording); the "please" cut (`CUT_WORD_SECS` and `start_closed_by_please` in `place`). The other cases the proposal lists are mechanisms, not words: "Restocked." is caught by a trigger the keyword spotter heard in the segment (`trigger_heard_in`), "okay, based" by a hearing at half the pause that holds no command alone (`said_alone`), "at 10am" by the last window heard while the utterance ran (`tail_heard_while_running`); they stay as code, their comments name the case only as an example.
- **The early head check:** `engine.rs::watch_running` and `hear_at_half_pause` ask `self.matcher.selects(STARTED_EARLY)`, i.e. infer from the selected phrases that the app is in standby.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** every engine, placed-command, voice-sample and voice control scenario passes unchanged; no mishearing word is matched by a branch in code; one declaration of the commands, their forms and their states, with tests that fail when `keywords.txt` or the app drift from it; `confirming.rs` split so that no file exceeds the file budget and `apply_answer`, `close_segment`, `place` and `watch_running` leave `size_baseline.toml`; `Session` within the struct budget.

**Non-Goals:** new commands or forms; any change of what is reported or when (the protocol version stays 2); the stall handling and queues of `command-spotting-stall-handling`; tuning on synthesized speech.

## Decisions

1. **`voice-commands` crate** (`voice-commands/`, workspace member, no dependencies): the seven command phrases; per command its forms (the words of each way to say it) and the states that accept it (`STANDBY`, `DICTATING`), `accepted_after(phrase)`; and the mishearing rows (decision 2). `command-spotting` takes `FORMS`, `STANDBY`, `DICTATING`, `accepted_after` and `ALIASES` from it, so `placed.rs` and `matcher.rs` keep only the matching. The app keeps its `VoiceCommand` enum and its `command_words.rs` (the Cyrillic forms are a property of the dictation transcript, not of command spotting) and takes the crate as a dev-dependency only: `app/tests/it/voice_commands/mod.rs` fails when `VoiceCommand::STANDBY`/`DICTATING` or `phrase()` differ from the crate, or when an exact form of a command that can reach a dictated text (the `DICTATING` commands and "start dictation") is missing from `command_words`' forms, which become a `pub const COMMAND_FORMS`. `command-spotting/src/keywords/tests.rs` fails when a phrase of `keywords.txt` is neither a declared command nor a trigger, or a declared command has no line. Alternative: a data file both read at build time — rejected, a crate gives types and needs no parser.
2. **Mishearings as data.** The crate holds `MISHEARINGS: &[Mishearing { heard, means, kind, recording }]`: `kind` is `Alias` (a phrase heard for the whole command: "presenter", "star dictation", "stuck dictation", "capsule dictation"), `NearMiss` (a short sentence ending in the word, heard for a quiet short form: "start" → "restart dictation"), or `AfterCommand` (words that close a chain: "and restart" → "press enter", "start dictation"); `recording` names the user's recording that produced it, where one exists (`session-2` at 27.23 s for "Let's start."). `matcher.rs` builds its aliases and near misses from the rows, `chain_from` its closing pair from the `AfterCommand` rows; `ends_with_and_restart` and its branch go. A `needs_voice_samples` + `needs_command_stt` test in `command-spotting/tests/it/mishearings/mod.rs` (a folder of its own: `tests/it` is over the directory budget) hears each row's recording through the speech recognition service for commands and asserts the matcher reads what it heard as the row's command (as a phrase or a near miss), so a row the service no longer produces still passes and a row that stops working fails. The alias rows feed both matchers, so the placed commands now also read "capsule dictation" as "cancel dictation", as the whole-utterance matcher always did: the one widening of this refactor, accepted because two lists of the same mishearings drifting apart is the defect this change removes.
3. **Trailing words as data.** `TRAILING_WORDS: &[TrailingWord { word: "please", cut_secs: 0.25 }]` in the crate: a word that may close a command and whose clip can be cut while it is still said. `place` asks the matcher whether the found start ends in a trailing word and takes its `cut_secs`; `CUT_WORD_SECS` and the literal "please" in `start_closed_by_please` go (`POLITE_WORD` becomes the rows' word).
4. **`words()` reads Unicode letters:** `char::is_alphabetic` and `to_lowercase`, so a Cyrillic transcript yields its words instead of none; no command is Cyrillic, so no Latin result changes. `JobKind::is_check` stays the one definition; `JobContext::is_check` becomes `self.kind().is_check()` through a `JobContext::kind`.
5. **`confirming/` directory**, one file per concern, each under 300 code lines: `mod.rs` (`AskedJob`, `JobContext` and its helpers, `head_command`, `spotted_command`, `starting_anywhere`, `timed`), `ask.rs` (asking, reserving, submitting, queueing, `poll`, `settle`), `answer.rs` (`apply`, `apply_answer` split into steps: the half-pause answer kept, the near miss, the stale check, the events, the early second hearing, hearing again, the tail, resolving), `segment.rs` (`ClosedSegment`, `close_segment` split into closing the utterance, the standing-in half-pause hearing, the span and the asking; `apply_segment`), `half_pause.rs` (`HalfPauseHearing`, its asking and dropping), `rehear.rs` (`Rehear`, `EarlyRehears`, the near-miss hearings), `place.rs` (`HeardClip`, `place`, the speech end after a trailing word, `tail_heard_while_running`), `unheld.rs` (`confirm_unheld`, `apply_unheld`). `hear_whole_instead` returns `Standing::{Applies(AskedJob), HeardWhole(Vec<ServerEvent>)}`. `apply` passes the matcher it reads with as a parameter (`reading: &PhraseMatcher`) down to `apply_answer`, `apply_heard` and the finders `place` calls, instead of swapping `self.matcher` with `mem::replace`; the jobs an answer asks in turn keep that matcher for their own answers, as the swap gave them. `place` takes a `HeardClip { heard, from, end: Option<usize> }` (none: the clip ran to the utterance's end) instead of `usize::MAX`, and `closing_word_of_start` is a free function of the matcher, as it reads no phrases.
6. **`Session` grouped** into the sub-states it holds: `Jobs` (`next_job`, `jobs`, `outbox`, `checks`, `spotted_checks`), `Hearings` (`heard_triggers`, `misheard`, `early`, `half_pause`, `stand_ins`), `Utterances` (`utterances`, `current`, `next_utterance`, `last_segment_end`), `Detector` (`vad`, `pause`, `vad_pending`, `vad_fed`, `vad_offset`, `samples_seen`), declared in `engine/state.rs`, so `Session` is at most 12 fields and leaves `size_baseline.toml`; `watch_running` hands the start checks to `check_head` and `ask_head`.
7. **Early start through the protocol.** `PhraseSelection` gains `#[serde(default)] early_start: Option<bool>`: whether the start of an utterance is searched early for a command opening speech. The app sends it (`true` when "start dictation" is among the commands it listens for); the engine uses it and only falls back to "start dictation is selected" when it is absent, so an older app and the tests that send phrases alone keep today's behavior. The answer travels with the phrases: `PhraseMatcher` holds `early_start` (`with_early_start`, `starts_early`), and `STARTED_EARLY` moves from the engine to `matcher.rs`, the fallback's one reader; `Session::select` takes the whole selection, `listen_for` stays for the phrases alone. One answer drives both early hearings of standby: the early head check and the hearing at half the pause. The protocol version stays 2.
8. **Cleanups on the way:** the stale doc comment above `raised` (it describes `save_speech`), `TRIGGERS_KEPT_SECS` and `LONGEST_CHAIN` moved to the constants at the file's top, the two `impl PhraseMatcher` blocks of `matcher.rs` joined. `accept` calls `ask_spotted_check` once per frame, after the answers are taken; when the first call is load-bearing (an `engine_it` test changes), it stays with a `WHY:` comment saying what it orders.
9. **A mishearing that cannot become a row** without changing behavior keeps its branch with a `WHY:` comment naming the case, and its fix is filed with `/create-change`; behavior stays.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The split reorders work inside a frame (job numbers, queue order)] → each move is mechanical; the engine and placed-command suites run after every group, and nothing is reworded in them.
- [A new workspace crate needs every check to know it] → `voice-commands` is added to `Cargo.toml` members, `stage_inputs` and `recognition_paths` in `.config/stages.toml` (it feeds command spotting), the `test-steps` members list and `.config/analyzers.toml` as other crates are.
- [The mishearing test needs the user's recordings and a service] → it lives in `needs_voice_samples::needs_command_stt` and runs in the checks that have both; the table's other uses are unit tests.
