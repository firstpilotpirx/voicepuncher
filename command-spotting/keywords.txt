# The phrases of voice control for the gigaspeech keyword spotting model: BPE tokens,
# then :boost, #threshold and @phrase, its words joined by _ (sherpa-onnx splits on spaces). Tokens come from the model's bpe.model:
#   uv run --with sentencepiece python3 -c "import sentencepiece as s; \
#     print(' '.join(s.SentencePieceProcessor(model_file='bpe.model').encode('PRESS ENTER', out_type=str)))"
# A phrase may have several lines: "press enter" runs together into "presenter", "start"
# may sound like "star", any phrase may end with "please", and "clear text" is "clear all text".
# A lower threshold or a higher boost makes a phrase easier to spot. press enter and clear
# all text are spotted less reliably than the dictation phrases, so they need the looser
# values; the app's pause rule (the phrase must be the whole utterance) guards them.
▁START ▁DI C T ATION :1.0 #0.25 @start_dictation
▁ST AR ▁DI C T ATION :1.0 #0.25 @start_dictation
▁START ▁DI C T ATION ▁P LE AS E :1.0 #0.25 @start_dictation
▁ST O P ▁DI C T ATION :1.0 #0.25 @stop_dictation
▁ST O P ▁DI C T ATION ▁P LE AS E :1.0 #0.25 @stop_dictation
▁CAN CE L ▁DI C T ATION :1.0 #0.25 @cancel_dictation
▁CAN CE L ▁DI C T ATION ▁P LE AS E :1.0 #0.25 @cancel_dictation
▁RE S T AR T ▁DI C T ATION :1.0 #0.25 @restart_dictation
▁RE S T AR T ▁DI C T ATION ▁P LE AS E :1.0 #0.25 @restart_dictation
▁PRE S S ▁EN TER :1.5 #0.20 @press_enter
▁PRE S ENT ER :1.5 #0.20 @press_enter
▁PRE S S ENT ER :1.5 #0.20 @press_enter
▁PRE S S ▁EN TER ▁P LE AS E :1.5 #0.20 @press_enter
▁PRE S ENT ER ▁P LE AS E :1.5 #0.20 @press_enter
▁C LE AR ▁ALL ▁T E X T :1.5 #0.20 @clear_all_text
▁C LE AR ▁ALL ▁T E X T ▁P LE AS E :1.5 #0.20 @clear_all_text
▁C LE AR ▁T E X T :1.5 #0.20 @clear_all_text
▁C LE AR ▁T E X T ▁P LE AS E :1.5 #0.20 @clear_all_text
