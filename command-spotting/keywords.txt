# The phrases of voice control for the gigaspeech keyword spotting model: BPE tokens,
# then :boost, #threshold and @phrase, its words joined by _ (sherpa-onnx splits on spaces). Tokens come from the model's bpe.model:
#   uv run --with sentencepiece python3 -c "import sentencepiece as s; \
#     print(' '.join(s.SentencePieceProcessor(model_file='bpe.model').encode('PRESS ENTER', out_type=str)))"
# Each command is said as one word, or that word twice. "enter", "clear" and "paste" are
# phrases of their own, named after their commands. "start", "stop", "cancel" and "restart"
# are only *_trigger lines, spotted in a stream of their own: a short "start" or "restart"
# asks the speech recognition service to check the running utterance at once, and any of
# them said twice in a row asks it about the doubled form at once. A lower threshold or a
# higher boost makes a word easier to spot; the checks of the utterance guard them.
▁EN TER :1.5 #0.20 @press_enter
▁C LE AR :1.5 #0.20 @clear_all_text
▁PA S TE :1.5 #0.20 @paste_text
▁START :1.5 #0.10 @start_trigger
▁ST AR :1.5 #0.10 @start_trigger
▁RE S T AR T :1.0 #0.25 @restart_trigger
▁ST O P :1.5 #0.12 @stop_trigger
▁CAN CE L :1.5 #0.15 @cancel_trigger
